Who you'll be diving with

I'm Anas Chair, an AIDA freediving instructor based in Berlin and the founder of Freediving Berlin.

I teach people to stay calm under water, from nervous first-timers to experienced divers chasing depth. My groups are small, unhurried and built around safety, because nobody relaxes while they feel rushed. Expect a lot of laughing between dives.

Away from the school, I work under water for film, television and the stage.
